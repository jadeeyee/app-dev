# My Favorite Movie: The Hunger Games :bow_and_arrow:

> "May the odds be ever in your favor."

## :pencil: About the Movie

**The Hunger Games** is a dystopian action film based on the novel by *Suzanne Collins*. It's set in **Panem**, a country divided into districts that are ruled by the wealthy **Capitol**.

Every year, each district has to send one boy and one girl, called *tributes*, to fight in a televised competition called the **Hunger Games**. When her younger sister is chosen, ***Katniss Everdeen*** volunteers to take her place.

## :purple_heart: Why I Love It

- The story is **exciting** and full of suspense
- Katniss is a *brave* and strong main character
- It has deeper messages about *power*, *courage*, *sacrifice* and *standing up for what is right*
- The costumes of the tributes are amazing
